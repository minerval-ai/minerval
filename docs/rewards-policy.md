# Contributor Rewards Policy

*Minerval, Inc.* · Not yet in force · Effective date: to be set

**In short.** Minerval sometimes offers to pay for a specific piece of
epistemic work: a challenge, evidence, an argument, a source, a merge or a
split that would improve the public claim graph. When Minerval accepts that
work as satisfactory, it pays a reward from its own funds. A reward is
payment from Minerval, Inc. for services rendered to Minerval. It is not a
contest prize, it is not a share of any funder's money, and it is not owed
until Minerval marks it payable at the end of a review period during which it
can still be reduced, denied, or reversed. Minerval uses automated systems,
including LLM agents, to make and evaluate offers. Those decisions are
provisional, and Minerval may correct them.

## 1. Scope and agreement

This policy governs every reward Minerval offers or pays for contributions to
the Minerval claim graph (the "Program"). It forms part of Minerval's Terms of
Service; where the two conflict about a reward, this policy controls. By
submitting a contribution to which an offer applies, or by accepting a reward,
you agree to this policy as in force at that time.

Nothing in this policy requires you to submit anything, and nothing about
contributing to Minerval changes because of the Program: sincere contribution
remains free, is never conditioned on a reward, and is governed by the
constitution and policies published on this site.

## 2. Definitions

- **Contribution**: any material you submit to Minerval through its
  contribution surfaces: a challenge, support, evidence, argument, proposed
  edit, merge or split proposal, claim proposal, or source.
- **Offer**: an invitation, recorded in Minerval's ledger and displayed on the
  site, to submit a Contribution of a described kind on a described claim or
  scope, together with the maximum amount Minerval currently expects to pay
  for fully satisfactory work.
- **Reward**: a payment by Minerval for a Contribution that Minerval has
  accepted as satisfactory under an Offer.
- **Automated Systems**: the software Minerval uses to run the Program,
  including large-language-model agents that survey the graph, recommend
  Offers and amounts, evaluate Contributions, review decisions, and initiate
  payouts.
- **Review Period**: the interval after acceptance during which a Reward
  remains Pending and may be reviewed, reduced, denied, or reversed.
- **Pending**, **Payable**, **Paid**, **Denied**, **Reversed**: the states of
  a Reward described in Section 6.
- **Payout Provider**: the third party through which Minerval sends payments,
  currently Stripe.
- **Funder**: a person or mandate that puts owls behind work on the graph, as
  described in Minerval's allocation documentation.

## 3. Rewards are payments from Minerval for work delivered to Minerval

3.1 **Payer and payee.** Every Reward is paid by Minerval, Inc. from
Minerval's own funds, to the contributor whose Contribution Minerval
accepted. The Contribution is made to Minerval and becomes part of the public
graph under the terms that govern all contributions. A Reward is
consideration for services rendered to Minerval and accepted to Minerval's
satisfaction, and for nothing else.

3.2 **Not a contest.** The Program is not a contest, competition,
sweepstakes, lottery, or game of any kind. There is no entry fee, and no
purchase is ever required or helpful: buying owls does not affect
eligibility, evaluation, or amounts. Rewards are not awarded by chance. No
fixed pool of money is set aside to be divided among entrants, and no one
"wins" against anyone else; Minerval may accept several Contributions under
one Offer, or none.

3.3 **Not wages.** Rewards are not wages or salary. Nothing in the Program
creates an employment, agency, partnership, or joint-venture relationship
between you and Minerval, or authorizes you to act for Minerval. You decide
whether, when, and how to do the work, and you may do the same or similar
work for anyone else. You are responsible for your own taxes, equipment, and
expenses.

3.4 **Not owls.** Rewards are stated and paid in money, never in owls. Owls
are prepaid, one-way service credits: they are never convertible to Rewards,
cash, or anything else, and Rewards are never convertible to owls. Accepted
Contributions may separately earn owls under Minerval's contribution award;
that award is a service credit, not part of any Reward, and is governed by
the account documentation rather than this policy.

## 4. Funders do not pay contributors

4.1 **What a mandate is.** A Funder who funds a mandate purchases scheduling
of Minerval's own work, which Minerval performs through its agents on the
public graph. A mandate is Minerval's statement, informed by the Funder's
objective, of which public-good epistemic work Minerval judges worth doing.
Minerval's Grantmaker agents may take a mandate's scope into account when
recommending which Offers to make and at what amounts. That is the whole of a
Funder's influence on the Program.

4.2 **What a Funder does not do.** A Funder does not employ, engage, direct,
supervise, evaluate, select, or pay any contributor, and does not decide
whether any Offer is made, its amount, whether a Contribution is accepted, or
whether a Reward is paid. No Funder's money is held for, earmarked to, or
passed through to any contributor. Funding does not create any relationship,
obligation, or claim between a Funder and a contributor, in either direction.
A Funder has no right to any Contribution and no say over it.

4.3 **Outcomes are never for sale.** No Offer is ever conditioned on a
Contribution reaching, supporting, or undermining any particular conclusion.
Offers invite evidence, argument, and structure, whichever way they cut.
Minerval declines mandates that seek to purchase outcomes, and an Offer that
would do so is void.

## 5. How Offers are made, and what an Offer is

5.1 **Automated Systems.** Minerval runs the Program largely through
Automated Systems. Agents survey the graph, decide which work to invite,
propose amounts within limits Minerval sets, evaluate Contributions, review
one another's decisions, and initiate payouts. Automated Systems can be
mistaken, and people may attempt to manipulate them. Every safeguard in this
policy exists for that reason.

5.2 **An Offer is an invitation, not a promise.** An Offer invites you to
submit work for Minerval's evaluation. It is not an offer to contract, not a
promise to pay any amount to any person, and not a guarantee that any
Contribution will be accepted. The amount displayed is the maximum Minerval
currently expects to pay for fully satisfactory work under that Offer.
Minerval may pay less than the displayed amount for work it judges partially
satisfactory, may divide an amount among several contributors, and may pay
nothing for work it judges unsatisfactory.

5.3 **Only recorded Offers are Offers.** An Offer exists only when it is
recorded in Minerval's ledger and displayed on the site with an amount and a
scope. Statements by an agent in conversation, chat, transcripts, notes,
messages, or anywhere else, and statements by any person not authorized in
writing by Minerval, are not Offers and create no obligation. No Automated
System has authority to bind Minerval beyond a recorded Offer, and no
recorded Offer binds Minerval beyond this policy.

5.4 **Changing and withdrawing Offers.** Minerval may modify, reduce, or
withdraw any Offer at any time before a Reward under it becomes Payable,
including after a Contribution has been submitted or accepted. Withdrawal
does not affect a Reward that is already Payable except as Section 7
provides.

5.5 **Automated decisions are provisional.** Every automated decision in the
Program, including the making of an Offer, the acceptance of a Contribution,
the amount of a Reward, and the initiation of a payout, is provisional.
Minerval may review, correct, or reverse any such decision, by a person or by
further automated review, at any time before payment and, under Section 7,
after. Minerval does not represent that any automated decision is correct,
and does not undertake to stand by any automated decision.

5.6 **Satisfaction.** Whether a Contribution is satisfactory is for Minerval
to decide, in good faith, applying the constitution and policies published on
this site, with the same standard whether or not an Offer applies. Acceptance
of a Contribution into the graph and acceptance for a Reward are related but
distinct: a Contribution may be accepted into the graph and still earn less
than the displayed amount, or nothing, if Minerval judges that it did not
deliver the work the Offer invited.

5.7 **Every Offer is backed.** Minerval sets aside a finite Program budget,
separate from and smaller than its general funds, and reserves the full
displayed amount of every Offer against that budget when the Offer is made.
The total of open Offers and of Pending and Payable Rewards never exceeds
the budget, so every Reward that becomes Payable can be paid even if every
open Offer is claimed at once. When the budget is fully committed, no new
Offers are made until an Offer closes, a Reward is Denied, or Minerval adds
to the budget. Individual Offers are kept small for the same reason.

## 6. The life of a Reward

6.1 **States.** A Reward moves through these states, each recorded on the
contribution record and visible on your account page:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Minerval has accepted the Contribution and recorded a proposed amount. The Review Period is running. Nothing is owed. |
| Payable | The Review Period has ended without denial or reversal, you are eligible, and Minerval's obligation to pay has arisen, subject to Section 7. |
| Paid | The Payout Provider has sent the payment. |
| Denied | Minerval decided, before the Reward became Payable, not to pay it, in whole or in part. |
| Reversed | Minerval withdrew a Payable or Paid Reward under Section 7. |

6.2 **Pending.** On acceptance, the Reward becomes Pending at the amount
Minerval determines. During the Review Period the acceptance may be appealed
by anyone, audited, superseded by later review or reassessment, or examined
for the matters in Section 7. A Pending amount is information about what
Minerval expects to pay if nothing changes. It is not a debt, a balance, or
property, and it may be reduced to any amount, including zero, during the
Review Period.

6.3 **Payable.** When the Review Period ends, if the Reward has not been
Denied and you are eligible, it becomes Payable. This is the moment at which
Minerval becomes obligated to pay, and the only such moment. The obligation
remains subject to Section 7 until payment is complete.

6.4 **Payout.** Payable Rewards are paid through the Payout Provider in
Minerval's next payout cycle after both of the following hold: your Payable
balance has reached the payout threshold, and you have completed onboarding
with the Payout Provider. A Payable balance below the threshold carries
forward. Minerval will pay out any Payable balance, whatever its size, at
least once in every twelve months, provided onboarding is complete.
Thresholds exist because the Payout Provider charges a fixed fee per payment;
they are not a way to withhold what is owed.

6.5 **Onboarding.** To receive a payment you must complete the Payout
Provider's onboarding, which includes identity verification, payout details,
and tax information, under the Payout Provider's terms. Minerval cannot pay
you until it is complete. A Payable Reward that cannot be paid because
onboarding is incomplete remains Payable for twelve months from the date it
became Payable, and Minerval will make reasonable efforts to remind you.
After that period, to the extent permitted by law, Minerval may treat the
Reward as forfeited.

6.6 **Program parameters.** The current values are published here and may be
changed by Minerval prospectively. As of the effective date they are:

| Parameter | Current value |
|---|---|
| Review Period | 30 days from acceptance |
| Payout cycle | Monthly |
| Payout threshold | 25 USD |
| Maximum Reward per Contribution | 100 USD |
| Maximum Rewards per contributor per calendar month | 500 USD |
| Program budget | A fixed amount Minerval sets aside and publishes here, initially 2,000 USD per month; every Offer reserves its full amount against it, and Offers pause while it is fully committed |

A change to a parameter applies to Rewards that become Payable after the
change. A Reward already Payable is paid on the terms in force when it became
Payable.

## 7. Holds, denial, reversal, and recovery

7.1 **Grounds.** Minerval may hold, delay, reduce, deny, or reverse any
Reward, in any state including Paid, and may recover any amount already
paid, where Minerval determines in good faith that any of the following
applies or is reasonably suspected:

- (a) the Contribution was not your own work, was copied, was submitted on
  someone else's behalf, or infringes or misuses anyone's rights;
- (b) the Offer, the acceptance, the amount, or the payout resulted from
  error, malfunction, or defect in Minerval's systems, or from an Automated
  System acting outside the limits Minerval set for it;
- (c) the Contribution, or any material connected with it, was designed to
  influence an Automated System other than on the merits, including
  instructions, prompts, or content aimed at the agents rather than at
  readers;
- (d) the acceptance was procured by collusion, coordinated activity,
  multiple or shared accounts, self-dealing, or participation by an
  ineligible person;
- (e) the acceptance was overturned on appeal, set aside on audit, or
  superseded by later review or reassessment that found the Contribution
  mistaken or not as represented;
- (f) you breached this policy, the Terms of Service, or any Minerval policy,
  or your account is suspended, restricted, or in a standing that requires a
  deposit to contribute;
- (g) you did not complete onboarding, identity verification, or tax
  documentation, or the Payout Provider declined, blocked, or reversed the
  payment, or sanctions, export-control, or anti-money-laundering rules
  apply;
- (h) paying the Reward would be unlawful, or would expose Minerval to tax,
  sanctions, or regulatory liability, or an investigation, dispute, or legal
  process concerning the Contribution or your account is open;
- (i) the Reward would exceed a Program parameter or budget in force when the
  Contribution was accepted.

7.2 **Who decides.** A determination under this Section may be made by an
Automated System or by a person, and a later determination supersedes an
earlier one. Where a determination reverses an earlier automated decision,
the later determination controls. Minerval records the ground on the
contribution record and notifies you.

7.3 **Recovery.** Where a Reward has been paid and Minerval reverses it, you
agree to repay the amount within thirty days of Minerval's request. Minerval
may also set the amount off against any Pending, Payable, or future Reward,
may cancel any owls credited as a contribution award for the same
Contribution, may ask the Payout Provider to reverse the payment, and may
refer suspected fraud to law enforcement. Minerval's rights under this
Section are cumulative and survive the end of the Program and of your
account.

7.4 **Holds pending review.** Where a ground in 7.1 is under review, Minerval
may hold the Reward in its current state until the review ends. Time spent
on hold does not count toward any period in this policy that runs in your
favor, and does not make a Reward Payable.

## 8. Amounts, currency, fees, and taxes

8.1 **Currency.** Rewards are stated in United States dollars and paid in
that currency or, where the Payout Provider requires it, converted to your
local currency at the Payout Provider's rate at the time of payment.

8.2 **Fees.** Minerval pays the Payout Provider's standard fee for a domestic
payment. Currency conversion, cross-border, intermediary bank, and
recipient-side fees may reduce the amount you receive, and Minerval does not
gross up for them. Minerval does not pay interest on any Reward.

8.3 **Taxes.** You are responsible for all taxes on Rewards. Minerval
collects the tax information the law requires through the Payout Provider's
onboarding, may withhold from a Reward where the law requires, may decline to
pay until required tax information is provided, and will file information
returns where required. Minerval does not give tax advice.

## 9. Eligibility

You are eligible to receive a Reward only if, at acceptance and at payment,
all of the following are true:

- you are at least 18 years old and able to enter a binding contract where
  you live;
- you hold exactly one Minerval account, controlled by you, in good standing:
  not suspended or restricted, not in a standing that requires a deposit to
  contribute, and with no unresolved bad-faith finding;
- the Contribution is your own work, or work you have the right to submit,
  and neither submitting it nor Minerval's use of it under the terms that
  govern contributions violates anyone's rights;
- you are not located in, ordinarily resident in, or a national of a country
  or region to which Minerval or the Payout Provider cannot lawfully send
  payment, and you are not on any sanctions or restricted-party list;
- you are not an employee, officer, or director of Minerval, a contractor
  engaged by Minerval, a person who operates, configures, or has privileged
  access to Minerval's Automated Systems, or a member of any such person's
  household;
- you can complete the Payout Provider's onboarding.

No fee, deposit, purchase, or payment of any kind is ever required to be
eligible. Minerval may restrict the Program by country or region at any
time. The Program is void where prohibited.

## 10. No guarantee, changes to the Program, and limitation of liability

10.1 **No guarantee.** Minerval does not guarantee that any Offer will remain
open, that any Contribution will be accepted, that any amount will be paid,
that any Automated System will function or decide correctly, or that the
Program will continue. The Program is offered as an experiment in paying for
public-good epistemic work, and Minerval may suspend, modify, or end it at
any time.

10.2 **On suspension or termination.** If Minerval ends or suspends the
Program: Rewards already Payable are paid on the terms then in force; Pending
Rewards complete their Review Period and become Payable or are Denied under
this policy; open Offers are withdrawn; and Contributions submitted after the
notice of termination earn no Reward.

10.3 **Limitation.** To the fullest extent permitted by law, Minerval's
entire liability arising from or connected with any Offer or Reward,
whatever the cause, is limited to the amount of that Reward as recorded in
Minerval's ledger, and Minerval is not liable for any indirect, incidental,
special, consequential, or punitive loss, or for lost profits, lost
opportunity, or reliance costs, including work you did in the expectation of
a Reward. Nothing in this policy limits liability that cannot be limited by
law.

## 11. Appeals and disputes

11.1 **Appeals.** If a Reward is reduced, Denied, or Reversed, you may appeal
through Minerval's contribution appeal process within sixty days of the
notice. The appeal is decided under the constitution and policies, may
itself be decided by an Automated System, and is subject to Section 7. The
appeal decision is Minerval's final decision under the Program.

11.2 **Disputes.** Any other dispute about the Program is governed by the
dispute provisions of Minerval's Terms of Service. To the extent the law
permits, you must raise a claim about a Reward within one year of the
decision you dispute, or it is waived.

## 12. Independence of the graph

Rewards buy work, never conclusions. Whether a Contribution is accepted into
the graph is decided on the same standard whether or not an Offer applies
and whatever the amount. No assessment, canonical form, importance score, or
other statement in the voice of the graph is ever affected by whether a
Reward was offered or paid. A Contribution that earned a Reward is marked as
such in its public contribution record, so that readers can see it. Minerval
will refuse, withdraw, or void any Offer that would compromise these
commitments.

## 13. Privacy

Onboarding information you give the Payout Provider is collected and
processed by the Payout Provider under its own terms. Minerval receives and
stores what it needs to run the Program: your verification and onboarding
status, a recipient identifier, the reward ledger, the tax information the
law requires Minerval to keep, and the records of decisions under this
policy. These records are kept as long as tax and legal obligations require,
which may be longer than the rest of your account. Minerval's Privacy Policy
describes the rest.

## 14. Changes to this policy

Minerval may change this policy at any time by publishing a new version on
this page with a new effective date. Changes apply to Rewards that become
Payable after the effective date; a Reward already Payable is governed by the
version in force when it became Payable. Material changes are announced on
this page. Continuing to submit Contributions under Offers after a change is
acceptance of the change.

## 15. Contact

Questions about the Program or a Reward: rewards@minerval.ai. Appeals go
through the contribution appeal process on the site.
