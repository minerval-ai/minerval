## Skills

### Costing (`skills/costing.md`, version 1, method skill)

How to estimate what a piece of work will cost before anyone pays for it: a request's budget, a scoping quote, a research run's ceiling, or a check on a budget someone else stated. A method skill: it belongs to no domain and is offered to every role that files, scopes, funds, or reviews requests. Use it when you put a number on work; it does not decide whether the work is worth doing, which is a judgment of value.

Sections: For every administrator; For the Grantmaker; For the Contribution Reviewer and the Dispute Arbitrator; For the researcher; Failure modes.

### Mathematics (`skills/mathematics.md`, version 1, domain skill)

How the constitution applies to propositions of mathematics: what a mathematical claim is, how proofs are arguments, how the six statuses and credence read for theorems and conjectures, what a machine-checked proof is as evidence, how formal statements are published and checked in Lean 4 against a pinned Mathlib, how the platform's solver and prize program work, and what money may never touch. Applies to claims tagged mathematics and to contributions on them. Does not apply to claims that merely use a number or a model.

Sections: For every administrator; For the Claim Steward; For the Grantmaker; For the Contribution Reviewer and the Dispute Arbitrator; For the Audit Agent; For the Curator; For the Matcher; For the Extractor; Standards for judging; Failure modes.

### Provenance (`skills/provenance.md`, version 1, method skill)

How to trace what a claim's support rests on: opening the sources behind a claim's instances and recording whether each source's own evidence bears the assertion it makes, which documents each assertion draws on and what survived the crossing, which sources are one voice appearing more than once, and a plain account for readers of what the support rests on, shown only where it matters. A method skill: it belongs to no domain and is carried on every run of the roles it addresses. It produces structure and figures for the Steward to reason about; the verdict stays the Steward's.

Sections: For every administrator; For the Claim Steward; For the Audit Agent; For the Curator; For the Extractor; For the researcher; Standards for judging; Failure modes.

### Search (`skills/search.md`, version 1, method skill)

How to search for evidence on a claim or question so that what the search did not find can be weighed: searching across framings and kinds of source, recording how the search was done, and reading absence in proportion to the search. A method skill; the verdict stays the Steward's.

Sections: For every administrator; For the researcher.

## Roles

### Extractor (`roles/extractor.md`)

Pulls atomic claims out of a source document, in canonical form.

### Matcher (`roles/matcher.md`)

The single decider of claim identity: does this proposition already exist (as itself, a rewording, or its negation)? Searches the graph itself.

### Contribution Reviewer (`roles/contribution-reviewer.md`)

Evaluates incoming contributions against policy — accept, reject, or escalate.

### Claim Steward (`roles/claim-steward.md`)

The owner of a claim: it decomposes the claim, maintains its canonical form, and assesses it over time. Its duty runs to the constitution and the health of the graph, not to any one contributor.

### Question Steward (`roles/question-steward.md`)

The owner of a question: it keeps the question's wording and presuppositions, gathers its candidate answers and subquestions, and gives the best answer it can with how well answered the question is.

### Curator (`roles/curator.md`)

The graph-level counterpart to the Steward. It owns the connective tissue between claims, merging duplicates and counterparts, splitting conflations, and suggesting cross-claim edges for Stewards to adopt.

### Dispute Arbitrator (`roles/dispute-arbitrator.md`)

Resolves escalations and appeals through careful adjudication, the highest-stakes governance call.

### Audit Agent (`roles/audit-agent.md`)

Quality control over the governance system itself: flags issues, adjusts reputation, suspends bad actors.

### Grantmaker (`roles/grantmaker.md`)

Designs and stewards funded mandates: surveys the territory, writes its mandate's valuations over the action ledger, grows its own plan, moves budget between peer mandates, and may refuse money that would warp the graph.

### Lookout (`roles/lookout.md`)

A standing watch a mandate funds: the cheapest agent with the narrowest question. Woken by a heartbeat or a trigger (the retraction poll, a poke), it reads its brief, the graph, the retraction record, and the open web, and raises candidates — a claim to reassess, a source to ingest, a note for its Grantmaker. It judges relevance, never truth, and can neither write an assessment nor move money.

### Consistency Checker (`roles/consistency-checker.md`)

The sweep that reads assessments against one another. Each Steward reads its own claim; the checker reads a region of the graph at a time for reasoning that conflicts with a neighbor's, evidence one claim records that another never weighed, and verdicts that are not a defensible function of what they rest on. It raises each find with the Steward whose claim looks wrong, as a reassessment the ledger prices and funds like any other; it writes no verdict and owns no claim.

### Solver (`roles/math-solver.md`)

The platform's own prover, an instrument rather than an administrator: it receives no constitution, owns nothing, and writes nothing to the graph. One bounded attempt on one published formal statement, with Lean, a computer-algebra sandbox, and a notebook; its report goes to the claim's Steward, who decides what it means.

### Researcher (`roles/researcher.md`)

An instrument an administrator launches for one bounded investigation: replicate a finding, trace a statistic to its origin, read and map a literature, check an inference. It answers only to its launcher, carries the constitution unless told otherwise, writes nothing to the graph but provenance rows, and returns a report the launcher weighs.
