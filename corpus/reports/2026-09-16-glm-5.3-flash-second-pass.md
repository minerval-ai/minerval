## What happened to the run

Seven stages launched in parallel at 22:42 UTC on separate corpus DBs. The
golden suite finished in 56 minutes and blackholes in 9 hours. At 08:02 UTC,
9 h 20 m in, **the sandbox VM was reclaimed** (a cloud session's VM is
released after a period of inactivity) and every other process died. The
databases survived on disk. What that left:

- **Every cluster had finished its Steward phase** (the caps were reached
  before the VM died); eggs, lableak and lethalities died while ingesting
  their remaining posts, so their graphs hold 2, 2 and 6 of 3, 5 and 11
  posts. They were scored as they stand the next day (`corpus:score` on the
  surviving DB, filed under `corpus/scorecards/<cluster>/2026-09-18…`).
- The **idempotency** and **path-independence** properties finished arm A
  (snapshotted) and died in arm B: no agreement numbers this pass.
- The **contribution scenario** had reviewed 2 of 10 when it died; it was
  re-run the next day on a restored copy of the path-independence arm A
  graph (a 5-Steward blackholes graph), see below.

The lesson is operational, not epistemic: **a multi-hour eval pass cannot
live in an interactive sandbox.** The merged `Corpus eval` workflow (#479)
runs each cluster as its own 6-hour-capped Actions job with open egress;
that is where the next pass belongs, once the three provider secrets exist.

## Problems found this pass (fixed on `claude/loving-allen-9sy8sy` unless noted)

1. **Steward ends with an empty turn and no assessment** (smoke run: one of
   two runs, 17 steps, empty `end_turn`). Fixed: the conditional final-tool
   nudge the reviewer uses now covers the Steward, only while no assessment
   is recorded.
2. **Forced structured call returns no tool call, `finish_reason` null**
   (blackholes post 3, after 297 s): reported as "model may not support tool
   calling", source lost. Fixed: retried twice, each attempt billed, before
   the error names the model; a truncation is still the caller's cap to raise.
3. **`terminated`** (undici, connection closed under the request) killed a
   Steward run and was not classified transient, so it counted as a failed
   attempt. Fixed: `terminated` and `fetch failed` are transient.
4. **Judge verdicts truncated at 8192 tokens**: 9 of 31 in the first scoring,
   each a paid call that scored nothing. Fixed: 32768; all four clusters were
   re-scored with the fix and lost no verdicts.
5. **Extraction truncated at 65536 output tokens** on two lethalities posts:
   GLM's reasoning on an 8k-word post can exceed 65k tokens. Not fixed; the
   knob exists (`EXTRACTION_MAX_TOKENS`) but at some point the model, not
   the cap, is the problem. Worth a look at whether the extractor prompt can
   ask for less deliberation.
6. **A multi-hour pass in the interactive sandbox dies with the VM.** Not a
   code problem; run long passes through the `Corpus eval` workflow.
7. **Stance `poses` on a concession** (golden `para-07`), above.
